Migrate a Village

موضع درآمد

Upload a ZALPORA-format Excel and the ID of its target Google Sheet. The sheet's six tabs (Khewats, Khatas, Khasras, Portions, Mutations, _Meta) will be overwritten. The target sheet must already be shared with the service account as Editor.

Where do I get the sheet ID?
  1. Create a blank Google Sheet (name it e.g. "Jamabandi — Zalpora").
  2. Click Share, add {{ service_account_email }} as Editor, uncheck "Notify people", Share.
  3. Copy the long string from the URL between /d/ and /edit. That's the ID.
